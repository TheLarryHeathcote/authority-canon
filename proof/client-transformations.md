# Proof — Client Transformations

## Client A: The Discounting Agent
**Context:** 5 years in business, consistently discounting commission by 0.5%–1% to win listings.
**Catalyst:** Realized the Mindset Tax was costing ~$15k/year in lost revenue.
**Method Applied:** Needs Audit, Role Shift.
**Outcome:** Stopped discounting, raised fees to market standard. Closed deals without lowering price.
**VERBATIM:** "I realized I was paying for their business with my own peace of mind."

## Client B: The Burned-Out Producer
**Context:** 40+ transactions/year, 70 hours/week, resenting clients.
**Catalyst:** Health scare and spouse ultimatum.
**Method Applied:** Values Audit, Boundary Defense, Micro-Routines.
**Outcome:** Cut volume to 25 transactions, hours dropped to 45/week, revenue stable.
**VERBATIM:** "I'm not working less; I'm working aligned. The clients respect the boundaries."

## Client C: The Negotiation Reset
**Context:** Anxiety during pricing conversations, defensive posture.
**Method Applied:** 15-Second Reset before pricing talk.
**Outcome:** Client accepted standard rate without pushback.
**VERBATIM:** "I used the 15-second reset before the pricing conversation. I didn't even have to defend my number; they just accepted it."
