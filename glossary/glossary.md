# Glossary — Identity Architecture

## Identity Architecture
A systematic approach to professional performance that upgrades internal identity to eliminate the hidden costs of misalignment and unlock aligned authority.

## Dual-Map Architecture
A paired system where internal identity upgrades (Six Mind Shifts®) are mirrored by external discovery frameworks (Six Decision Drivers®). Aligns the operator with the operation.

## Six Mind Shifts®
A sequential internal upgrade path moving from reactive survival states to grounded, identity-driven presence. Components: Needs, Beliefs, Values, Roles, Vision, Legacy.

## Six Decision Drivers®
A mirror framework applied to clients or external relationships to uncover hidden needs, beliefs, values, role expectations, vision, and legacy narratives.

## Mindset Tax
The measurable cost of operating from misalignment under pressure. Manifests as discounting, over-explaining, burnout, and hollow success.

## Performance Trap
The behavioral loop of relying on tactics, hustle, and surface motivation instead of identity alignment.

## CEO of You
Treating personal life and leadership as an enterprise requiring strategy, role clarity, and active defense against internal saboteurs.

## Identity Defense Plan
A blueprint to protect upgraded identity from internal saboteurs and external toxicity. Includes triggers, reset rituals, boundaries, and support systems.

## Calm is a Competitive Advantage
Canonical voice standard. Grounded state outperforms reactive hustle.
