# Framework — Mindset Tax & Performance Trap

## Definition
The measurable cost of operating from misalignment under pressure. The Performance Trap is the behavioral loop that sustains it.

## Components
- **Performance Trap:** Reliance on tactics, hustle, and scripts instead of state calibration.
- **Mindset Tax:** Financial and emotional drain from misalignment (discounting, over-explaining, burnout).
- **State Misalignment:** Root cause of tactical failure under pressure.

## Application
Diagnostic tool to quantify the hidden drag of misalignment and justify identity work over tactical fixes.

## What It Replaces
The belief that "more hustle" or "better scripts" solve performance gaps.

## Downstream Use
Keynote hook, article stack anchor, entry-point diagnostic, and conversion messaging.
