# Framework — Commission Breath

## Definition
The physiological anchor of the 15-Second Reset; a deliberate breath pattern that signals safety to the nervous system and locks in the upgraded identity state.

## Components
1. **Inhale (Protection):** Draw in grounded state.
2. **Hold (Conviction):** Lock the upgraded identity.
3. **Exhale (Release):** Drop desperation and tactical urgency.
4. **Entry (Aligned Authority):** Speak from the new state, not the old survival loop.

## Application
Used inside the 15-Second Reset, the Driveway Reset, and immediately before fee or negotiation conversations. Physiological override of the fight-or-flight response.

## What It Replaces
Shallow breathing under pressure, physiological panic, and tactical scrambling born of nervous system misalignment.

## Downstream Use
Signature story anchor, video demonstration asset, and coaching ritual.
