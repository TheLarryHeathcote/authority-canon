# Framework — Identity Defense Plan

## Definition
A blueprint to protect upgraded identity from internal saboteurs and external toxicity.

## Components
1. Top 3 Triggers or Saboteurs
2. Reset Rituals (e.g., 15-Second Reset)
3. Boundaries I Will Defend
4. Emergency Response Plan
5. Support Circle

## Application
Post-audit maintenance. Ensures identity upgrades survive high-pressure environments and prevent regression to the Performance Trap.

## What It Replaces
Fragile mindset shifts that collapse under stress or external pressure.

## Downstream Use
Coaching maintenance module, workshop blueprint, and client retention tool.
