# Framework — 15-Second Reset

## Definition
A micro-routine to interrupt the Performance Trap and recalibrate state before high-stakes interactions.

## Components
1. **Pause:** Physical and mental stop. Break the momentum of reaction.
2. **Commission Breath:** Physiological anchor that signals safety to the nervous system.
3. **Identity Lock:** Recall the upgraded role ("I am the CEO of You").
4. **Entry:** Step into the interaction from aligned authority, not tactical need.

## Application
Deployed before pricing conversations, after rejection, or pre-call. Takes 15 seconds. Prevents the Mindset Tax from compounding in real-time.

## What It Replaces
Reactive responses, defensive posturing, and rushing into tactical scrambling.

## Downstream Use
Workshop exercise, daily practice tool, and conversion hook for entry-point diagnostics.
