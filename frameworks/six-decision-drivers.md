# Framework — Six Decision Drivers®

## Definition
A mirror framework applied to clients or external relationships to uncover hidden needs, beliefs, values, role expectations, vision, and legacy narratives.

## Components
1. Client Needs
2. Client Beliefs
3. Client Values
4. Client Role Expectations
5. Client Vision
6. Client Legacy

## Application
Used in client discovery, negotiations, and leadership conversations to map the other party's internal architecture. Eliminates guesswork.

## What It Replaces
Transactional pitching, surface-level objection handling, and reactive sales tactics.

## Downstream Use
Sales training, brokerage leadership programs, negotiation modules, and team culture workshops.
