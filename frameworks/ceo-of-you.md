# Framework — CEO of You

## Definition
Treating personal life and leadership as an enterprise requiring strategy, role clarity, and active defense against internal saboteurs.

## Components
1. **Role Statements:** Clear declarations of who you are operating as in a given context ("In this role, I choose to...").
2. **Strategic Rhythm:** Operating on a defined cadence instead of reacting to market noise.
3. **Boundary Defense:** Non-negotiable standards for time, energy, and fees.
4. **Saboteur Interception:** Recognizing and neutralizing the internal voice that defaults to survival mode.

## Application
Pre-interaction calibration and ongoing operational leadership. Used to anchor fee conversations, reject misaligned clients, and maintain standards under pressure.

## What It Replaces
Winging it, apologizing for standards, and operating from survival instead of strategy.

## Downstream Use
Keynote anchor, book chapter foundation, and executive coaching module.
